
# Electric Vehicle Market Analysis

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes the electric vehicle market to understand EV sales, adoption trends, vehicle types, manufacturers, and market performance. The dashboard provides interactive insights into the growth and distribution of electric vehicles.

---

## Objective

- Analyze electric vehicle sales and market trends
- Understand EV adoption patterns
- Compare different vehicle types
- Analyze manufacturers and their market performance
- Identify trends in electric vehicle growth
- Generate meaningful business insights

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization

---

## Key Analysis

- Total Electric Vehicles
- EV Sales
- EV Adoption Trends
- Manufacturer-wise Analysis
- Vehicle Type Analysis
- Year-wise EV Growth
- State/Region-wise Analysis
- Market Share Analysis
- Electric Vehicle Trends

---

## Key Insights

- Analyzed the growth of electric vehicle adoption
- Compared EV performance across manufacturers
- Identified popular electric vehicle types
- Examined year-wise market trends
- Analyzed the distribution of electric vehicles across regions
- Identified important patterns in the EV market
